01 What to expect

Pre-qualified sellers and a disciplined protocol.

Most small-company introductions stall on missing records, unverified recurring revenue, or an unprepared founder. Every introduction is screened against five quantitative gates before it reaches your desk.

01

Pre-qualified targets

Normalized EBITDA with a documented add-back schedule, recurring revenue mix, customer concentration check, and founder transition alignment verified in advance.

02

3-day target clearance

Before scheduling introductions on a buy-side mandate, we submit candidate names in writing for a 3-business-day conflict check to confirm no existing active dialogue.

03

Dedicated first-look window

When we introduce a proprietary off-market founder under a buy-side mandate, your team receives a dedicated first-look window until you either advance or pass.

04

100% written-first

Initial blind profiles, mutual NDAs, clearance checks, and preliminary Q&A run asynchronously in writing by email, with zero calendar friction.

05

Exclusivity on clear terms

Granted against a written LOI that settles price, structure, earn-out metrics, the working capital approach and the founder's role after completion.

06

Single-side success fee

No retainers and no monthly fees. On a buy-side origination mandate, you pay a standard completion fee at closing with a 24-month protection tail. On a sell-side mandate, the seller pays our fee. Never both.

03 Who goes forward

What founders ask of an acquirer.

Founders care deeply about who buys the firm they built over twenty years. The acquirers we partner with can demonstrate four things.

  • A comparable track record. Dated acquisitions of similar firms, and a clear strategic thesis for the target.
  • The capital to complete. Committed institutional backing, a permanent-hold balance sheet, or verified sponsor dry powder.
  • A named Corporate Development or M&A lead. And prompt written responses on target clearance and IOI timetables.
  • Discipline after the LOI. Protecting team culture and local brand, with any price adjustments justified solely by new diligence findings.

05 Acquisition criteria

Tell me what you would buy next.

A few lines by email are enough to establish a buy-side finder mandate or register your acquisition criteria.

  • The firms you buy. MSP, ERP/CRM ecosystem (Sage, Acumatica, NetSuite, Dynamics, Salesforce), or vertical software niche.
  • Geography and size floor. Target US states or UK regions, minimum revenue, and minimum adjusted EBITDA.
  • Structure & model. Permanent-hold vs sponsor cycle, full buyout or majority recapitalization.
  • Who decides. Head of M&A, VP of Corporate Development, or CEO contact for 3-day target clearance.

Criteria are kept strictly confidential. You will not be added to any mass mailing list.
