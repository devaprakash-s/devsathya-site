mail-mcp privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

mail-mcp is a private tool that Dev Sathya runs on his own computer. It lets the AI assistant he works with search, read and draft email in mailboxes he owns. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.

What it can access

When the owner of a Google account signs in and agrees on Google's consent screen, mail-mcp can:

  • read messages, threads and labels in that Gmail account (gmail.readonly), and
  • save new drafts in it (gmail.compose).

mail-mcp has no send function. Drafts stay in the Drafts folder until the account owner reviews and sends them himself.

How the data is used

Messages are fetched only when the account owner asks his assistant for them, and are used only to answer that request: finding a message, summarising a thread or preparing a draft. To do this, the content is passed to the AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) working in that session.

mail-mcp does not keep copies of messages, does not run on any server, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone else. The data is never used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

mail-mcp's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Running node auth.mjs remove <address> deletes the stored sign-in from the computer.
