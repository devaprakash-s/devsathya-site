About

Dev Sathya

Independent M&A adviser for enterprise technology firms in the United States and the United Kingdom, and an architect of enterprise platforms. In enterprise technology since 1998.

01 Background

Two halves of one career.

I founded my first technology company in 1998 and have been building ever since. Before that I served on the Tata launch team for India's first GSM mobile network, after cutting my teeth managing enterprise and regional dealer networks on the ground across Karnataka. At Dell I helped scale India operations from 17 to 750 people and led the transition of Dell's USD 4 billion UK consumer business from Ireland to Hyderabad, followed by running offshore operations in Hyderabad for a 2,500-person firm serving regulated US and Australian institutions. While living in Greensboro, North Carolina (2006 to 2012), I built and ran a 20-engineer Bangalore product center shipping US clinical software. Between 2012 and 2016 I commercialized template-free pattern-recognition and machine-learning document intelligence across insurance, logistics, healthcare, and BPO - a decade before generative AI - and over the last 18 months I have designed, coded, and deployed 12 production enterprise AI platforms solo.

Advising owners, global boards, and institutional platforms brings both halves together. Whether structuring an enterprise technology acquisition across the US and UK or stewarding a cross-border engineering hub, subsidiary, or enterprise rollout across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, and India's Tier-2 industrial corridors, I evaluate businesses the way an operator and principal architect does.

I work directly with principals and say so plainly. The person who writes to you qualifies the mandate, audits the architecture and normalized schedules, manages conflict clearance, and stays at your side through completion.

I operate remotely from India (with deep roots across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Pune) alongside our US mailing office at 1178 Broadway, 3rd Floor #3433, New York, NY 10001, USA. Every engagement runs across US, UK, and European business hours, and in writing first: a clear, asynchronous written record removes calendar friction and forms the backbone of disciplined execution.

03 Principles

How I work.

Single-side fee alignment

The practice is compensated by one side only, never both. On a sell-side mandate the owner pays the success fee; on a buy-side origination mandate the acquiring platform pays the introduction fee at zero cost to the seller.

Paid on completion on M&A

On M&A mandates, there is no upfront retainer, no monthly maintenance fee, and no charge for an initial valuation or buy-box qualification. Transaction fees are earned exclusively at closing.

100% written-first & confidential

Every qualification step, conflict check, and NDA runs asynchronously in writing by email, without calendar friction or preliminary phone calls. No target identity is ever disclosed without prior written consent.

Evidence before narrative

Every figure in a buyer summary or information memorandum traces to a primary financial or technical record that confirmatory diligence can verify.
