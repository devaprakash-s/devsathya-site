04 The difference

An M&A adviser who reads the technology the way an acquirer does.

In an enterprise technology firm much of the value sits where a generalist adviser cannot see it: how durable the managed services contracts are, how far the firm depends on one vendor's partner programme, whether its own IP is an asset or a liability, and how much revenue survives a change of control.

I have designed, built and run enterprise technology since 1998. I use that to find what a buyer's diligence will find before the buyer does, and to present the strengths in the terms acquirers use to price them.

  • Recurring revenue quality. Managed services, support renewals and subscriptions separated from project work and licence resale.
  • Vendor and partner position. Tier, certifications, and whether partner status transfers on a change of control.
  • Technology and IP. Own products, add-ons and integrations, code quality and technical debt.
  • Customer and contract risk. Concentration, renewal terms and change-of-control clauses.

06 Process

A controlled, written-first process.

Whether we introduce you directly to a contracted permanent-hold buyer or run a multi-bidder process, every stage runs in writing with records that hold up in confirmatory diligence.

  1. Days 1 to 5

    Written Check

    Four headline numbers confirmed by email under a 1-page Mutual NDA. Zero call friction.

  2. Weeks 1 to 3

    Clearance

    Normalized earnings schedule, blind profile, and 3-day buyer conflict clearance.

  3. Weeks 3 to 8

    Introduction

    Direct double opt-in introduction (first-look) or coordinated multi-buyer wave.

  4. Weeks 6 to 12

    Offers (IOI/LOI)

    Written valuation ranges compared and negotiated into a signed Letter of Intent.

  5. Weeks 10 to 18

    Diligence

    Exclusivity with the winning buyer, VDR management, and confirmatory diligence.

  6. At the close

    Completion

    Purchase agreement execution, funds wired at closing, and orderly handover.

08 Principles

How I work.

Single-side fee alignment

I am compensated by one side only, never both. On a sell-side mandate the owner pays the success fee; on a buy-side origination mandate the acquiring platform pays our introduction fee at zero cost to the seller.

Paid on completion only

There is no upfront retainer, no monthly maintenance fee, and no charge for an initial valuation or buy-box qualification. Compensation is earned exclusively when a transaction closes.

100% written-first & confidential

Every qualification step, conflict check, and NDA runs asynchronously in writing by email, without calendar friction or preliminary phone calls. No target identity is ever disclosed without prior written consent.

Evidence before narrative

Every figure in a buyer summary or information memorandum traces to a primary financial or technical record that confirmatory diligence can verify.
