Questions

Before you write.

Will you need my passwords?

No. You invite me to your code (GitHub, or the project in your AI tool) and create a test login for me. You can remove my access the moment the job is done.

Do I have to go ahead with the fix?

No. The report is yours to keep. Fix it yourself, with another developer, or with me. The $600 only comes off the price if I do the fix.

What if the app cannot be saved?

The audit says so plainly, and compares the cost of fixing it with the cost of rebuilding the weak parts. Most apps are worth saving, because the hard part, knowing what to build, is already done.

Will you change my app while you audit it?

No. The audit reads and tests, it does not change anything, and it never touches your real customers' data. Before I test the live app I ask you to add a small verification file, so I only ever test an app whose owner has said yes in writing.

Can I keep building with Lovable or Bolt afterwards?

Yes. I work in your own project and explain every change, so you can carry on prompting. The handover notes tell you which parts to leave alone.

Is my code safe with you?

I sign an NDA if you want one, work on a copy wherever I can, and delete what I hold when the job ends. The code is yours throughout.
