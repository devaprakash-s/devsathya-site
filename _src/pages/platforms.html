01 The thesis

Enterprise software is slow because of handoffs, not typing.

A strategist writes a requirement. An architect turns it into a design. A team writes the code. Another team runs it. Every handoff loses information and adds a queue. I remove the handoffs by doing all four.

01

Remove the handoffs

One person owns the thesis, the architecture, the build and the operations. Nothing gets lost between people, because there is no between.

02

Replace them with rules

Locked contracts and enforced boundaries instead of coordination meetings. The build itself refuses anything that breaks the rules.

03

Keep the discipline

Phase gates, written records and honest scope, so nothing is lost to speed. Every decision survives after I am gone.

03 Platforms and value

Where platform work meets enterprise value.

A platform is worth what it lets a business do, prove and sell. The same discipline runs through the advisory work.

  • Vendor technology diligence. For owners preparing a sale: the technology estate reviewed as an acquirer's team would review it.
  • Platform strategy. Where to build, buy or consolidate, written as a thesis with the reasoning and the case against.
  • Governed AI systems. Model output can only propose a typed action; a person with the right permission executes it. Every model call passes one audited gate.
  • Claim discipline. A written record of what the system does, kept apart from what would sell better.
