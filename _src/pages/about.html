About

Dev Sathya

Independent M&A adviser for enterprise technology firms in the United States and the United Kingdom, and an architect of enterprise platforms. In enterprise technology since 1998.

01 Background

Two halves of one career.

I founded my first company in 1998 and have been building ever since. Before that I was part of the team that launched India's first GSM mobile service with Tata. Later I ran Dell's four billion dollar UK consumer business from India. Between 2012 and 2016 I worked in image recognition, long before it was fashionable. More recently I have designed, built and operated enterprise platforms end to end as their sole architect.

Advising owners and institutional platforms on enterprise technology acquisitions brings the two halves together. An enterprise technology firm is valued on things I have spent decades building and running: recurring contract revenue, platforms, partner ecosystems and the technical teams that make them work. I read a firm the way an acquirer's diligence team will, and I use that to position the business and defend the valuation.

I work directly with principals and say so plainly. The person who writes to you qualifies the business, prepares the normalized schedules, manages target clearance and coordinates the process through to completion alongside your legal and tax counsel.

Our US mailing address is 1178 Broadway, 3rd Floor #3433, New York, NY 10001, USA. Every engagement is run across US and UK business hours, and in writing first: a clear, asynchronous written record removes calendar friction and forms the backbone of a disciplined transaction.
